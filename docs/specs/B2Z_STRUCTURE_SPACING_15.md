# Rules15 structure spacing

Rules15 is a separate successor to Rules14. It preserves Schema7, Adapter9 / `BTZPAY06`, prices, Shout semantics, ownership, physics, and one-tick execution. Rules13 and Rules14 continue to apply their original friendly-proximity policy to their own records.

A new Cannon or Diverter site, or a relocation of either, must be at toroidal Chebyshev distance **at least four** from every other Cannon and Diverter and from any enemy-owned territory. Use the lesser wrapped distance on each axis and take the greater of those two distances. Axial and diagonal distances of 3 are rejected, including across either seam. Distance 4 satisfies spacing. A relocation excludes only the moved structure's typed ID. It still cannot use its existing cell or any occupied cell. The pre-existing minimum of two between Diverters remains an invariant of grandfathered worlds. It is subsumed by the four-tile admission check for a new site. Reorient and Fire have no destination change.

Additional construction still uses an explicit owned eligible site on an active island. Apply existing occupancy, closed ball footprints, topology, anti-stranding, prices and capacity checks. Site validation occurs at execution. A paid rejection produces an ordinary retained-payment receipt with no Shout. The observer's placement outlines and quotes use the exact authenticated configuration and do not reserve cells.

Automatic Join still uses the existing deterministic maximin search and retained patch, occupancy, and topology checks. The candidate cannon centre must also clear every retained structure and all occupied territory by four tiles. A player with no retained cannon cannot own an anchored island, so no owned-territory exception is required. A width-seven neutral neighbourhood around the chosen centre is necessary. A world with fewer than 49 neutral cells cannot contain such a site. An unsuccessful search preserves the existing `NO_ELIGIBLE_SPAWN` receipt. Historical sites remain in place, even if they would fail admission today.

The frontend is compatible with the certified Rules14 and Rules15 configurations. Rules15 destination search uses cancellable, cooperatively scheduled toroidal rings ordered by distance and canonical cell ID. A no-site result is explicit. An exhaustive no-site check may cover the world across multiple slices.

The controller uses `prepare_spacing_transition`, `spacing_transition_status`, and `commit_spacing_transition(expected_previous_manifest)`. Use the normal state-preserving upgrade while the exact Rules14 predecessor remains active. The transition holds admission, drains the official Ledger/Index cutoff and seals the current predecessor before committing authenticated metadata v11. At commitment the Schema7 bytes, adapter, official source binding, cursor and historical records are preserved. The first persisted preparation adds a state record outside the Rules14 strict decoder. From that point recovery must use the normal Rules15 module or a separately qualified compatible forward repair.
