# Cannon and Diverter manufacturing

Manufacturing is a local observer presentation for explicit new Cannon and
Diverter placement. It uses the same procedural base and upper assembly as
the verified structure, with canonical heading, cell and toroidal projection.
It uses the construction payer's accent. The original approved film reference
and complete laser instructions were unavailable. The prelaunch design uses
two graphite drones, payer-coloured beams with pale cores, and a local luminous
scan on the exact geometry.

## Payment and presentation

- An editable placement proposal uses the existing placement preview.
- A successful wallet copy may show a local planned construction for the exact
  saved proposal. Its financial status remains “no payment sent” by the game.
  The activity entry can hide the local preview.
- Optional signed payment uses the existing journal. Submitting, unresolved
  and Ledger-confirmed intents can show an indeterminate preview. The optional
  journal's exact receipt verification gates any verified outcome or entity
  association. The renderer never initiates or retries payment.
- A successfully matched outcome can remain pending in presentation until the
  displayed entity arrives. A displayed structure on the cell displaces a
  local manufacturing preview even if payment association is unavailable.
- The first automatically placed Cannon has no preselected target. Other
  actions use their own existing presentation.

The progress bar is indeterminate. Local waiting neither establishes
checkpoint timing nor reserves construction space. The displayed state
remains the only authority for geometry picking, collision, ownership and
progression. A financial receipt never jumps the presentation clock.

The observer shows up to two local sites in the verified foreground view at
construction zoom. Reduced motion retains a static scene and progress state.
Visual plans expire after 15 minutes. This presentation deadline leaves the
financial journal, recovery, and payment evidence intact. A stale or missing
receipt supplies no invented result. For supported Rules19 deployments the
prelaunch and public frontends share the same presentation.
