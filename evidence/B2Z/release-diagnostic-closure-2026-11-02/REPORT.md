## Next tactical/economic experiment actually executed

```
node tools/b2z_matched_strategies.mjs .work/economic-defence --economic-defence
```

Twenty paid comparisons share four immutable Wait controls (24 executions/rows).
The same bent bridge, both endpoint/account seats, 0.25 ICP defender ceilings,
fixed opposing inputs and sixteen logical ticks are matched within each group.
Explicit elapsed inputs assume Conserve's five seconds/tick. Proactive Move and one
supporting ball are admitted at tick 0; reactive Move at ticks 2/12, and a supporting
or recovery ball at tick 12. Historical pending strikes are excluded only in this
explicit synthetic setup, as in the accepted reactive experiment.

The attacker chooses either the cardinal connection or a different diagonal
connection. Each is one ball and costs 0.1001 ICP including the fixture fee, so this
comparison removes the earlier two-direction attack's higher expenditure. There
are no real Ledger calls. [Exact scores, receipts, costs, birth/death attribution,
state/input hashes and measurements](economic-results.json).

| Fixed attack | Candidate | Incremental final territory | Outcome |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Cardinal | Proactive Move | +4 cells at both seats | Large short-horizon score benefit for 0.0002 ICP |
| Cardinal | Reactive Move, 10/60 seconds | 0 | Rejected after ownership/lifecycle changes; payment retained |
| Cardinal | Proactive supporting ball | 0 | Small score benefit; the purchased ball dies within the horizon |
| Diagonal connection | Proactive Move | 0 | No survival benefit; one seat has a small negative score delta |
| Diagonal connection | Proactive supporting ball | +1 cell at both seats | Purchased ball dies instead of the original support; one friendly ball survives |
| Diagonal connection | Supporting/recovery ball after 60 seconds | +7 cells at both seats | Paid revival retains one live supporting ball |

All ball-purchase variants cost 0.1001 ICP including fee; every Move costs 0.0002
ICP including rejected moves. Wait spends zero. Full expenditure, not just
amount_used, is counted. Zero-spend score efficiency is null, never infinity.
Canonical accrued score minus the matched Wait score is primary; natural ongoing
accrual is recorded separately. Exact ratios are preserved as integers/rationals,
not floating-point money calculations. These are score-efficiency measures, not
ICP profit or payout promises.

The runner reuses the reviewed canonical birth/death accounting. It counts a
purchased ball that dies within the same horizon rather than inferring casualties
from net populations. Every paid candidate has one canonical terminal receipt;
every opposing attack has an applied launch receipt. The two connections and both
seats have identical budgets/inputs within their respective matched groups.

Measured total: 28,229 cursor work units / 38.293 seconds. The existing 2,000,000-work
and 60-second simulation deadline are unchanged. These are local cursor/time
measurements, not billed IC cycles. No forecast engine, policy weights, hosted
service or new spending authority was introduced.

**Finding:** cheap timely cannon defence is effective against this predictable
cardinal threat, but an equally priced attack on another connection defeats it.
Additional support or paid recovery can preserve a position where movement fails,
at roughly 500 times the per-action cost. Waiting preserves ICP. The small selected
fixture, fixed headings, retained handcrafted initial velocities and sixteen-tick
horizon do not establish a universal policy ranking or justify a price/rule change.
Future opponent follow-up purchases are unknown. This local tick-12 experiment
does not make the ordinary eight-tick live forecast's unassessed delays robust.
