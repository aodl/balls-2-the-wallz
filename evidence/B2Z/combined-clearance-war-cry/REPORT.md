## Production recovery boundary

The new optional durable transition field is absent before preparation. Once
preparation writes it, an old strict Root decoder is not an approved blind rollback.
After the authenticated epoch, a Rules7-only backend/source decoder is categorically
unsafe. Recovery must preserve state and use a qualified compatible forward module;
never reinstall/reset, reverse an epoch, reindex paid inputs or manufacture refunds.
See [separate release decisions](RELEASE_DECISIONS.md).
