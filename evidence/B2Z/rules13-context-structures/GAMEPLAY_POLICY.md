# Rules13 gameplay policy

Construction and relocation require an active, owned destination and a different friendly cannon or Deflector at toroidal Chebyshev distance 1–3. The search is a 7×7 neighbourhood, including both seams and diagonals. The moving structure, including its old cell, cannot qualify the move. Anchors on other islands belonging to the same player qualify. The destination still has to pass its own active-island checks.

Enemy-owned cells at distance 0–3 disqualify a destination. At distance 4 the clearance check passes. Neutral land and separate islands of the payer are not enemies. Dormant enemy cannon tiles count. A sole cannon needs another friendly structure before it can relocate.

Additional cannon purchases require an explicit, active, owned, unoccupied cell. Owned cells may be more than one tile from a cannon. Neutral adjacent cells and implicit additional NEW are rejected. Automatic first Join retains its previous placement, price and receipt behaviour. Live-ball closure, including exact edges and corners, and existing capacity, ownership and island-stranding checks still apply.

Deflector construction and relocation retain spacing of at least two between Deflectors. Relocation preserves identity, orientation and ownership. Reorientation preserves the cell and skips the new placement checks. It rejects an unchanged direction. Both operations preserve cannon-only territorial anchoring, direct-capture immunity and automatic removal on committed supporting-territory loss.

Authoritative legality is evaluated when the payment is processed. A territory change or structure loss can invalidate an earlier preview. Historical placements, commands, receipts and journal entries retain their original meaning.
