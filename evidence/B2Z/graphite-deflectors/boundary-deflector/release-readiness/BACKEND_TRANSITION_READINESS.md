## Recovery limits

For either transition, the first persisted preparation record is a durable compatibility boundary. Old strict Root decoders cannot be assumed to handle it. The exact Rules11→Rules12 test explicitly rejected a downgrade to 5ffb6cc2 after preparation, retained the current module and state, and recovered with the exact normal successor. Tests also reload the same normal artifact during admitted Rules12 work and reconstruct the resulting certified chain, paid state and receipts. Rules11's own ordered rehearsal also exercised same-module recovery of prepared state. These qualify recovery for a compatible module reinstall in **upgrade mode** after transient interruption or loss of executable service, subject to the actual failure and a separate maintenance decision. They provide no alternative module for a deterministic bug in the selected normal runtime. Such a defect requires a separately qualified forward repair before recovery. Check any proposed rollback before the first preparation against the exact durable decoder and state.

After epoch commit, preserve the successor decoder, adapter, world, journal, receipts and complete authenticated chain. The Rules12-compatible 81-file freeze supports an asset restore. A defect within those frontend bytes requires separately qualified compatible frontend bytes. Stop on an unexplained journal, certified head, receipts or cutover discrepancy. Avoid reinstalls, resets, stale snapshots, cursor rewind, Ledger reindex and fabricated refunds.
