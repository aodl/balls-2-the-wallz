## Deflector engine checkpoint — exact reason no backend candidate is offered

The required mechanic is an absolute outgoing heading at a path-dependent
interior contact, not mirror reflection, immediate tile-edge turning, capture or
centre teleportation. Current schema6 uses integer global intercepts. The exact
same-speed(5,12)→(13,0) illustrative vector contacts at1/48 at(5/48,3/4), requiring
new x intercept−1/6tile, or−512/3 at1024units/tile. It cannot be stored by the old
integer schema without altering position. Old schema/validators/historical hashes
were therefore preserved.

The JS/Rust native checkpoint agrees exactly with11 independent Fraction oracle
vectors for contact, time and outgoing intercept. It also has explicit tangent/
corner-only negatives. These are unit-cell geometry examples, not canonical
speed normalization or authenticated game events. A four-device abstract2×2-torus
experiment shows denominator growth29/283/1417/4252bits after10/100/500/1500 positive
turns. This is not a certified1000×1000 world, universal impossibility result or
Zeno proof; it rejects treating naive unbounded rational anchors as a proven
bounded design. [Contact parity](results/contact-parity.json) and
[negative growth](results/numeric-growth.json) retain that scope.

A versioned fixed-point/anchored alternative still needs exact rounding,
cell-sidedness, equal-time order, cumulative behaviour and resource/progress proof,
then coordinated JS/native state, durable scheduler, validator, animation and
migration implementation. No arbitrary floating rounding, silent quotas, wall-clock
cooldown, lost events or stuck-ball deletion was substituted. A stronger simulated-
time/distance rearm policy is still an engineering decision to prove, not a request
for routine owner permission.

Consequently there is no real DEFLECT parser/normalized adapter, processing-time
purchase, occupied-site integration, removal-on-loss lifecycle, redirect event,
verified piecewise trail, dual authenticated observer, valueless Ledger/Index
Deflector transaction, every-work-unit recovery or Rules11→Deflector migration.
The distinct Build deflector action and Deflect & Pay workflow are also incomplete;
keeping them unavailable under Rules7/11 is safety, not their implementation.
