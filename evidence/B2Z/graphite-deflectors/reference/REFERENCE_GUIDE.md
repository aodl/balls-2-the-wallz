# Reference guide — inspect the PNGs; do not treat generated art as a physics specification

## Precedence

1. Owner requirements and `CODEX_PROMPT.md` determine gameplay, layout, financial safety and tile alignment.
2. `04_APPROVED_PANEL_STRUCTURE.png` determines the panel's information architecture and axes.
3. `01_APPROVED_VISUAL_LANGUAGE.png` determines the shared material/accent language.
4. `02_APPROVED_CANNON.png` and `03_APPROVED_DEFLECTOR.png` determine the selected model families.
5. `05_CURRENT_UI_BASELINE.png` and `06_CURRENT_CANNON_BASELINE.png` are actual prior-game evidence only.

The PNGs have been copied byte-for-byte from the supplied conversation assets. `REFERENCE_MANIFEST.json` records their original filenames, roles, sizes, dimensions and hashes. No new mockup or alternative model was created for this package.

## 01 — Approved visual language

A graphite, bevelled, lightly translucent action HUD; pale owner-colour emissive details on gunmetal structures; off-white text; cool informational note; small player-colour variants.

**Implement:** coherent materials and modest depth, understated luminous accents, readable dark structures, crisp active indicators, consistent copy/person/action icon style.

**Do not literally implement:** giant hero-scale HUD, catalogue/footer, duplicate action-button set, four-direction-only arrows, hard-coded lime for all players, giant glowing selection squares on every idle structure, real bloom/shadow/reflection costs, or the header's extra decorative cannon icon. The central divider positions in this illustrative sheet are not the exact shared axis required by the owner.

The white-ball trails in the illustration communicate motion. They are not new paired projectiles or always-on beams. Financial strings/prices in any image are illustrative; quote actual certified values.

## 02 — Approved cannon

Faceted graphite turret, chamfered base, bright restrained muzzle ring, recessed dark muzzle, armoured collar, three or four readable broad armour planes and sparse accent strips. This is the refined continuation of the owner's selected armoured cannon, not the alternative spherical design.

Keep the broad silhouette at normal game scale. The base sits squarely in one canonical cell. The upper assembly rotates without rotating the base. All horizontal vertex extents, including the muzzle at arbitrary headings, must fit the tile; the drawing alone is not proof of footprint correctness.

The base in the concept is not a request to add new cannon collision or occupancy rules. Physical cannon behaviour remains unchanged.

## 03 — Approved Deflector

A shallow square chamfered plate and low raised triangular wedge/arrowhead with a fine luminous directional edge. The whole base aligns with the world grid; the top can point in any supported direction.

The ball/path depiction represents incoming and outgoing states, not two simultaneous copies of one ball. Use a continuous piecewise path at a verified contact, not teleportation. The tile-wide effect need not literally collide with the visible triangle; a mesh-only collider would miss some entrants and is not the requested guarantee.

Avoid the earlier round-base Deflector, a floating cursor triangle, a tall tower, four arrow sprites or a permanent laser emitter.

## 04 — Approved panel structure

The owner explicitly approved this arrangement:

- Title | exact amount + Copy.
- From + abbreviated account + person | To + abbreviated recipient + Copy.
- Memo + complete command + Copy.
- Calm blue informational note.

The two vertical dividers align; the three Copy buttons align. `From` and `To` sit to the left of their text. No faux input boxes. No From Copy action. About five visible principal characters plus an ellipsis; copying is always the full canonical value.

Keep that structure while applying the newer material language. Add the distinct Deflector action to the contextual icon toolbar rather than growing another modal or footer. The first image's footer does not supersede this constraint.

## Capture requirements for the implemented result

Provide actual application captures at the same camera/zoom/viewport/owner colour, not an image-generated replacement. Include a model turntable/contact sheet generated by the actual renderer plus in-arena normal-scale views. Document the distinction between controlled geometry fixtures, authenticated disposable games and live production observations.

The acceptance comparison is: recognisably the approved art, clearly seated on tiles, readable at real scale, and measured to be inexpensive. Pixel-perfect reproduction of physically expensive soft light or exaggerated artistic bloom is not the target.
