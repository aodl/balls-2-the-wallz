# Graphite panel finish

The natural-size comparison is a pixel crop of the actual 1440×900 Fire panel
before and after, with the same source cannon, memo and amount. The left frame
was served by the production origin. The right frame used the frozen candidate
over loopback with the same authenticated European backend. The crop does not
change the scale or appearance of either capture.

The clipped lime fragment at the upper-left join is gone. A single subdued
rail edge replaces the thick dark gap. The footprint uses one thin neutral
outline and one recessed lower inlay, inset from the rounded bottom corners.
The Copy and person controls use a shallower common bevel sized to the same
44px targets. The ready price, active action and calm blue notice remain
distinct. The same graphite treatment applies to the secondary workspace
without new control rows or overlays.

The cannon and renderer art are unchanged. The payment split, Copy column,
status-only panel footprint, local editor clearance and single scroll area
remain covered by the browser regressions. There is no broad extra glow or
