Verification and operation

   
Select a cannon to inspect it. Declare your sending account to find your own.
Fire, Move and Build prepare instructions for your wallet. Only ICP Ledger payments issue commands; the site never sends them.
Ledger payments are real and do not reserve tiles. Changing a proposal leaves any payment already submitted to the Ledger unchanged. An action can be rejected during processing. Rejected and unused amounts are retained without automatic refunds. The action amount excludes the wallet’s Ledger fee. Shouts are public on-chain text.
Territory earns scores. Automatic payouts and refunds are unavailable.
