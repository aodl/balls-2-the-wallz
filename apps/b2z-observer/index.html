Rules

Send an ANGLE transfer from your existing wallet to start a cannon or add a ball. MOVE relocates an existing cannon to an owned cell. Funding accounts identify players; selecting a cannon is local inspection.

Balls capture territory and reflect at verified collisions. Territory earns logical area-time credits. A cannon losing its last ball is eliminated; earned credits remain.

Join: 1 ICP. Additional balls: 0.1 ICP × active count squared. Move: 0.0001 ICP. Always copy the current authenticated amount from the action card. Fees are separate. Payments are retained, with no refunds or automatic payouts.
