About this demonstration and its limits

The recordings are produced by the existing BTZ v3 reference kernel and timeline coordinator. State, audit events and scores remain unchanged. The compact animation is checked against its predecessor and resulting snapshot before playback.

A complete recording is finite. At its end, the observer stops rather than silently inventing another round. Restart begins a new local replay. Input parsing, ledger authority, certificates, payouts and production performance are not demonstrated here.

One recorded logical tick is presented as 0.25 seconds at 1×, before small buffer corrections. Territory colors and glow are presentation only. Reduced-motion preferences start playback paused.
